Version 1.0 is the first release of Notchmark.

• Library rail of Articles with Amortizing and Profit phases
• Notch logs a use and carves one share from the balance bar
• A Breakeven Mark is written when the balance reaches zero
• Ledger ranks Marked Articles by Profit per notch
• Forecast to Mark for still-Amortizing Articles
• Library grades A+ through F, relative to this library
• Add Article with Price ÷ target uses or Manual share
• Scan a price tag to prefill purchase price
• Settings for USD, EUR or GBP, CSV export of notch history, Contact us, Re-run onboarding, and Reset all data
• On-device storage only
